Maintenance Bulletin: Reagent Dosing System

1 Overview

The reagent dosing system supplies xanthate from the xanthate tank to the roughing cell through a metering pump and a flow meter. The controller adjusts the pump speed to keep the reagent dosage stable.

2 Known faults

Table 1. Faults of the reagent dosing system.
FaultCauseAction
Dosage too lowMetering pump wearReplace the pump diaphragm
Dosage unstableAir in the dosing lineBleed the dosing line
Flow meter reading driftsScaling on the sensorClean the flow meter

3 Calculation

Dosage (g/t) = reagent flow (g/min) / ore feed rate (t/min) (1)

A low reagent dosage reduces the recovery rate, and an excess reagent dosage makes the froth viscous and lowers concentrate grade.
